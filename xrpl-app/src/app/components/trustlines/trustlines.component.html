Trust Line Requirements

Trust Lines allow your account to hold issued currencies (IOUs) from a specific issuer.

🔗 Create / Modify Trust Line
LimitAmount.currency ✅ Required
LimitAmount.issuer ✅ Required
LimitAmount.value ✅ Required
Flags (NoRipple, Freeze, etc.) ➕ Optional
❌ Remove Trust Line
LimitAmount.value = 0 ✅ Required
Outstanding Balance ⚠️ Must Be 0
No Active Freeze ⚠️ Required
📝 Important Notes:
  • Trust Lines are required to hold issued tokens (IOUs). XRP does not require a trust line.
  • Setting a positive limit creates a trust line if one does not already exist.
  • Reducing the limit does not remove the trust line unless the balance is zero.
  • To fully remove a trust line, the balance must be zero and the limit must be set to 0.
  • Each trust line increases your account’s owner reserve while it exists.
  • The issuer may freeze a trust line if they have the Freeze feature enabled.
