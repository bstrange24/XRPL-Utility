Sending XRP (Direct Payment)

A direct XRP Payment transfers XRP from one account to another quickly and cheaply. It can also fund a new account if sending enough XRP to meet the base reserve requirement.

💸 Basic XRP Send Most Common
Destination ✅ Required
Amount ✅ Required
DestinationTag ❌ Optional
💸 With Extras Advanced Options
SendMax ❌ Not needed for pure XRP
Paths ❌ Not needed for direct XRP
LastLedgerSequence ➕ Recommended (expire safely)
📝 Important Notes:
  • Destination and Amount (in drops; 1 XRP = 1,000,000 drops) are required for a basic XRP send.
  • Use DestinationTag when sending to exchanges or hosted wallets (e.g., required by Binance) to identify the recipient.
  • Common fields like Fee and Sequence are usually auto-filled by clients/libraries.
  • Transactions are irreversible — double-check the address and tag before signing.
  • Sending at least the base reserve (~10 XRP, check current via server_info) to an unfunded address creates a new account.
  • Fees are very low (typically <0.00002 XRP) and burned — no refunds.

⚠️ Irreversible & Safety Tips

XRP Payments cannot be reversed or canceled once submitted.

  • Wrong Destination or missing DestinationTag can result in permanent loss of funds.
  • Always verify the recipient address carefully — XRPL addresses are case-sensitive and checksummed.
  • For time-sensitive sends, include LastLedgerSequence to prevent hanging in the queue indefinitely.
  • If sending to an exchange, confirm their exact tag requirements to avoid crediting issues.
