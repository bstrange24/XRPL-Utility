Time-Based Escrow Requirements

Time based Escrows lock XRP, or an IOU, until a specified time.

⏱️ Unlock Only No Expiration
FinishAfter ✅ Required
CancelAfter ❌ Optional
⏱️ Unlock + Expiration With Cancel Time
FinishAfter ✅ Required
CancelAfter ➕ Optional
📝 Important Notes:
  • FinishAfter is required for time-based escrows.
  • If CancelAfter is provided, FinishAfter must be earlier than CancelAfter.
  • Each escrow increases your account’s owner reserve until it is finished or canceled.

⚠️ No Cancel Option (Irreversible Escrow)

If the escrow does not include a CancelAfter time.

  • The escrow can never be canceled by the sender.
  • Funds will remain locked until the escrow is finished.
  • If the destination cannot or does not finish the escrow,
    the XRP may remain locked permanently.
