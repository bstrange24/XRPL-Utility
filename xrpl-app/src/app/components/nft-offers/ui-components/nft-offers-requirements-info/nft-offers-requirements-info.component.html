NFT Offers (Buy / Sell / Cancel)

Create sell offers (list your NFT for sale), buy offers (bid on someone else's NFT), accept offers, or cancel your own active offers.

How NFT Offers Work

Create NFT Offer
NFTokenID Required
Amount Required
Offer Type Sell or Buy
Expiration Optional
Destination Optional (direct offer)
Accept Cancel
NFTokenOfferIndex Required
NFTokenID (for accept) Required when accepting
BrokerFee Optional

Important Notes

  • Sell offer: You must own the NFT; it becomes locked until accepted, canceled, or expired.
  • Buy offer: You place a bid on an NFT you don't own; the seller must accept.
  • Accepting a sell offer transfers the NFT to you and pays the seller (minus any TransferFee if set).
  • Only the creator of an offer can cancel it.
  • Offers consume owner reserve (~0.2 XRP) until resolved.
Important Risks
  • Once accepted, the transfer is permanent.
  • Sell offers lock your NFT during the offer period.
  • Wrong NFTokenID or OfferIndex will cause the transaction to fail.
  • Always double-check IDs and test on Devnet/Testnet first.
