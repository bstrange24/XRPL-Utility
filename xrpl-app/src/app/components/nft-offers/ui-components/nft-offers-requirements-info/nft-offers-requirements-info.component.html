NFT Offers (Buy / Sell / Cancel)

Create sell offers (list your NFT for sale), buy offers (bid on someone else's NFT), accept offers (complete a purchase), or cancel your own active offers using NFTokenCreateOffer, NFTokenAcceptOffer, and NFTokenCancelOffer transactions.

💰 Create Offer NFTokenCreateOffer
NFTokenID ✅ Required (32-byte hex ID of the NFT)
Amount ✅ Required (XRP drops or issued currency amount)
OfferType ✅ Sell (your NFT) or Buy (bid on another's)
Expiration ➕ Optional (ledger close time or relative seconds)
Destination ➕ Optional (specific buyer for direct offers)
✅ Accept / Cancel Offer NFTokenAcceptOffer or Cancel
NFTokenOfferIndex ✅ Required (offer ID to accept or cancel)
NFTokenID (for accept) ✅ Required when accepting
BrokerFee (optional) ➕ Optional (small XRP fee to broker)
📝 Important Notes:
  • Requires the NonFungibleTokensV1_1 (or later) amendment to be enabled on the network.
  • Sell offer: You must own the NFT; offer locks it until accepted, canceled, or expired.
  • Buy offer: You bid on an NFT you don't own; seller must accept to complete transfer.
  • Amount: Can be XRP (drops) or issued currency (with issuer). For sell offers, if tfOnlyXRP was set at mint, only XRP is allowed.
  • Expiration: Ledger close time (absolute) or relative seconds from now — expired offers auto-remove without fee.
  • Accepting a sell offer transfers the NFT to you and pays the seller (minus any TransferFee royalty if set at mint).
  • Accepting a buy offer transfers the NFT from you to the bidder and receives payment.
  • Cancel: Only the creator of the offer can cancel it — frees the NFT (if sell offer) and removes from ledger.
  • Offers consume **owner reserve** (~0.2 XRP per active offer) until accepted, canceled, or expired.
  • BrokerFee: Optional small XRP amount paid to a third-party broker account on accept (useful for marketplaces).

⚠️ Irreversibility & Offer Risks

Offers lock NFTs or funds until resolved — mistakes can lead to lost opportunities or unintended transfers.

  • Once an offer is accepted, the transfer is **permanent** — NFT moves and payment is made; no native reversal.
  • Sell offers lock your NFT — if you cancel or it expires, you regain control, but during the offer period, you cannot burn/transfer it elsewhere.
  • Wrong NFTokenID or OfferIndex causes transaction failure — double-check IDs before submitting.
  • If TransferFee was set at mint, seller receives less than Amount on accept — factor royalties into pricing.
  • Expired offers auto-cleanup, but active offers consume reserve — too many can lock up your account's XRP unnecessarily.
  • Destination (direct offer) restricts who can accept — if set incorrectly, offer may never be taken.
  • Always test offer creation, acceptance, and cancellation on devnet or Testnet first — wrong amounts, expirations, or IDs burn fees without effect.
