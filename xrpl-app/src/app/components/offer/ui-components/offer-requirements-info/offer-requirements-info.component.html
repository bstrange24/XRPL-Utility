XRPL DEX Offers

Create, cancel, and manage offers on the XRPL decentralized exchange to trade any two assets.

Key Operations

Create DEX Offer
Taker Gets Required
Taker Pays Required
Flags Optional
(Default Passive)
Cancel Offer
Offer Sequence / Index Required for cancel
Order Book Query TakerGets + TakerPays pair

Important Notes

  • Offers are limit orders that sit in the order book until matched, canceled, or expired.
  • Taker Gets: What you receive (what you're buying into).
  • Taker Pays: What you give (what you're selling).
  • Common flags: tfPassive, tfImmediateOrCancel, tfFillOrKill, tfSell.
  • Each active offer consumes owner reserve (~0.2 XRP) until resolved.
Risks & Considerations
  • Once matched, trades are permanent — no native reversal.
  • Active offers lock reserve — too many can freeze your account's XRP.
  • Wrong asset pair or issuer leads to invalid offers.
  • Always test on Devnet/Testnet first.
