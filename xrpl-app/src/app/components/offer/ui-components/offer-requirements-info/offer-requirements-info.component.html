XRPL DEX Offers (Create / Cancel / Order Book)

The XRPL decentralized exchange (DEX) lets you create offers to trade any two assets (XRP ↔ issued tokens or token ↔ token). Use OfferCreate to place buy/sell orders, OfferCancel to remove your own active offers, and query order books or your offers to monitor the market and your positions.

📈 Create Offer OfferCreate
Taker Gets ✅ Required (asset you want to receive)
Taker Pays ✅ Required (asset you offer to give)

Flags ➕ Optional (passive, immediate-or-cancel, fill-or-kill, sell)
❌ Cancel / View Offers OfferCancel or Query

📝 Important Notes:
  • Offers are limit orders on the XRPL DEX — they sit in the order book until matched, canceled, or expired.
  • Taker Gets: What the counterparty receives (what you are selling / buying into).
  • Taker Pays: What the counterparty pays (what you are buying / selling from).
  • Common flags:
    • tfPassive — Offer does not consume existing offers (good for market making)
    • tfImmediateOrCancel — Execute only what can be filled immediately; cancel rest
    • tfFillOrKill — Fill entire amount or cancel completely
    • tfSell — Treat as sell offer even if amounts suggest buy (useful for IOUs)
  • Each active offer consumes owner reserve (~0.2 XRP) until canceled, fully consumed, or expired.
  • Cancel uses your offer's sequence number (from account_offers) — only you can cancel your own offers.
  • Offers auto-match when a new offer crosses the book — partial fills are possible; unmatched portion stays active.
⚠️ Irreversibility & DEX Risks

Offers lock funds or assets until resolved — market volatility, wrong pricing, or unfilled offers can tie up reserve or cause losses.

  • Once matched, trades are permanent — no native reversal.
  • Wrong TakerGets/TakerPays pair or issuer causes offer to be invalid — double-check currency codes and issuers.
  • Active offers consume reserve — too many unfilled offers can lock your account's XRP.
  • Always test on devnet or Testnet first before submitting to mainnet.
