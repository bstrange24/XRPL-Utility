Automated Market Maker (AMM)

AMMs on XRPL enable decentralized liquidity pools for trading between any two assets (XRP or issued tokens). Create pools, deposit/withdraw liquidity, swap assets via the pool, clawback issued tokens from the pool, or delete empty pools using AMMCreate, AMMDeposit, AMMWithdraw, AMMClawback, and AMMDelete transactions.

TradingFee ➕ Optional (0–1000 = 0–1% in 0.001% steps; set at create)

📝 Important Notes:
  • Requires the AMM amendment (XLS-30) to be enabled on the network.
  • Each AMM pool is identified by its unique AMM ID (hash of Asset1 + Asset2 + issuer(s)).
  • TradingFee: Set once at creation (0–1%); liquidity providers earn proportional share of fees on swaps.
  • Depositing liquidity mints LP tokens to your account — these represent your share of the pool and can be withdrawn or traded.
  • Withdrawing burns LP tokens and returns proportional assets from both sides of the pool (or one side with exact flags).
  • Swaps use Payment tx with AMM path — specify exact input/output or min received; slippage protection via limit fields.
  • Clawback: Only the issuer of one asset can clawback their tokens from the pool (useful for stablecoins or regulated assets).
  • Deleting an AMM requires zero outstanding LP tokens — all liquidity must be withdrawn first; deletes the pool object and frees reserve.
  • Each AMM pool and each holder's LP tokens consume **owner reserve** (~0.2 XRP per object) until removed.

⚠️ Irreversibility & Liquidity Risks

AMM pools lock funds in shared liquidity — impermanent loss, fee misconfiguration, or clawback can affect returns or access.

  • TradingFee is **permanent** once set at creation — choose carefully (high fees discourage trading; low fees reduce LP rewards).
  • Depositing creates LP tokens that track pool share — impermanent loss occurs if asset prices diverge significantly.
  • Clawback removes issuer tokens from the pool — can disrupt liquidity or force LPs to withdraw early if not anticipated.
  • Deleting an AMM is **irreversible** — pool gone forever; ensure no users hold LP tokens or the tx fails (tec codes like tecOWNERS).
  • Swaps are **instant but irreversible** — no native slippage protection beyond your min received; large trades can suffer high impact.
  • Reserve costs add up with many pools or large LP holdings — monitor owner reserve to avoid account freeze-out.
  • Always test full flows (create → deposit → swap → withdraw → clawback → delete) on devnet or Testnet first — wrong asset pairs, amounts, or fees burn tx fees without effect.
