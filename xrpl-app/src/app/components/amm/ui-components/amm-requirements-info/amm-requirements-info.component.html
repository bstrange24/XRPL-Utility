Automated Market Maker (AMM)

AMMs on XRPL enable decentralized liquidity pools for trading between any two assets (XRP or issued tokens).

Key Operations

Create / Manage Liquidity
Asset 1 + Asset 2 Required
Amount 1 +
Amount 2 Required
(for create/deposit)
Trading Fee Optional (0–1%)
Swap Clawback Delete
AMM ID Required
Amount (for swap/clawback) Required

Important Notes

  • Requires the AMM amendment (XLS-30) to be enabled.
  • Each AMM pool is uniquely identified by its AMM ID.
  • TradingFee is set once at creation (0–1%) and cannot be changed.
  • Depositing liquidity mints LP tokens representing your share of the pool.
  • Swaps are instant but subject to slippage.
  • Clawback allows issuers to remove their tokens from the pool.
  • Deleting an AMM requires zero outstanding LP tokens.
Risks & Considerations
  • Impermanent loss can occur if asset prices diverge.
  • TradingFee is permanent — choose carefully.
  • Many pools can consume significant owner reserve.
  • Always test full flows on Devnet/Testnet first.
