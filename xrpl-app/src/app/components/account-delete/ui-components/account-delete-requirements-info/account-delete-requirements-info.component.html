AccountDelete

AccountDelete permanently removes an XRPL account from the ledger and transfers any remaining XRP (after burning the deletion fee) to a destination address.

Requirements

🗑 Account Delete
Destination Required
Deletion Fee Required (auto-burned)
Amount Remaining XRP (auto-transferred)
🔒 Ledger Preconditions
No owned objects Trust lines, offers, checks,
escrows, NFTs, etc. must be cleared
No Tickets TicketCount must be 0
Destination must exist Funded XRPL address
Ledger waiting period 256 ledgers (~15-20 min) after last tx

🧹 Recommended Cleanup Before Deletion

  • Remove all trust lines and settle IOU balances
  • Cancel all open DEX offers
  • Finish or cancel escrows, checks, and payment channels
  • Burn or transfer all NFTs and MPTs
  • Remove signer lists, credentials, permissioned domains, etc.

📝 Important Notes

  • The transaction burns a special deletion fee (much higher than normal fees).
  • Remaining XRP is automatically sent to the Destination address.
  • The destination must already exist on the ledger.
  • Many exchanges do not accept funds from AccountDelete — avoid sending to exchange addresses.
  • Transaction will fail with tecOWNERS if any owned objects remain.
⚠️ Permanent & Irreversible Action
  • Once deleted, the account address can never be recreated or reused.
  • If the destination is wrong, the XRP may be permanently lost.
  • The burned deletion fee is permanently removed from circulation.
  • Always test the full cleanup + deletion process on Testnet first.
