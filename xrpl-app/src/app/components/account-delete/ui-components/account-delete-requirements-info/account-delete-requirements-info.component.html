Account Deletion (AccountDelete)

AccountDelete permanently removes an XRPL account from the ledger and transfers any remaining XRP (after burning the required deletion fee) to a destination address. This is typically used to reclaim reserve XRP from unused or test accounts.

No Tickets ✅ TicketCount must be 0
Destination funded ✅ Must already exist on the ledger
Ledger waiting period ✅ Must wait 256 ledgers (~15–20 minutes) after the account's previous transaction

Recommended cleanup before deletion

  • Remove trust lines or settle any outstanding IOU balances
  • Cancel open DEX offers
  • Finish or cancel escrows, payment channels, and checks
  • Burn or transfer NFTs and MPT tokens
  • Remove signer lists, hooks, DIDs, credentials, and permissioned domains
📝 Important Notes
  • The transaction burns a special deletion fee (currently much higher than normal fees).
  • After the burn, the remaining XRP is automatically sent to the destination account.
  • The destination must already exist and meet the base reserve requirement.
  • Many exchanges do not accept AccountDelete transfers, so sending to exchange deposit addresses can result in lost funds.
  • All owned ledger objects must be removed before deletion or the transaction will fail with errors such as tecOWNERS.

⚠️ Permanent & Irreversible Action

Account deletion permanently removes the account from the ledger.

  • Once confirmed, the address can never be recreated or reused.
  • If the destination address is incorrect or unsupported, the transferred XRP may be permanently lost.
  • The burned XRP (deletion fee) is permanently removed from circulation.
  • Always test the cleanup and deletion sequence on Testnet or Devnet before performing it on Mainnet.
