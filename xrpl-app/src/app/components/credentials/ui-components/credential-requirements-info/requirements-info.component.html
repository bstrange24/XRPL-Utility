Issue Credential

Issuers create credentials to attest facts about a subject (KYC status, membership, age verification, compliance approval, etc.). The credential exists on-chain but must be accepted by the subject before it becomes active.

🪪 CredentialCreate Issuer Action
Subject ✅ Required
(address receiving credential)
CredentialType ✅ Required
(issuer-defined identifier)
Data / URI ➕ Optional
(metadata or pointer)
Expiration ➕ Optional
(credential expiry date)
📝 Important Notes
  • Issuer pays the owner reserve (~0.2 XRP) when creating.
  • The credential is inactive until the subject accepts it.
  • CredentialType should be unique per issuer (e.g. KYC-2025).
  • Credential contents are public on the ledger.
} @case ('accept') {

Accept Credential

The subject accepts a credential issued to them. Once accepted, the credential becomes active and the owner reserve shifts from the issuer to the subject.

✅ CredentialAccept Subject Action
Credential ID ✅ Required
(ID created during issuance)
📝 Important Notes
  • Acceptance activates the credential.
  • The owner reserve shifts to the subject.
  • The credential becomes permanently associated with the subject address.
} @case ('delete') {

Delete Credential

Credentials can be removed from the ledger under certain conditions depending on whether they were accepted.

🗑 CredentialDelete Issuer or Subject
Credential ID ✅ Required
📝 Important Notes
  • Deleting frees the owner reserve.

Verify Credential

Any third party can verify a credential by checking the ledger for the credential object and confirming the issuer, subject, and credential type.

🔎 Ledger Verification Read Only
Credential ID ✅ Required
Issuer Optional check
Subject Optional check
📝 Important Notes
  • Verification requires only a ledger lookup.
  • Credentials are public and cryptographically verifiable.
  • Applications can enforce rules such as requiring specific issuers or credential types.
