Account Deletion (AccountDelete)

AccountDelete permanently removes an XRPL account and all its owned objects from the ledger, returning any remaining XRP (after burning fees and reserves) to a specified destination address. Use this to clean up unused or test accounts and reclaim reserve XRP — but only when you're certain the account is no longer needed.

Destination ✅ Required (funded r... address)
Amount ❌ Not specified (all remaining XRP sent)
Fee ✅ Must cover burn of at least owner reserve
🔒 Preconditions for Deletion Strict Requirements
No owned objects ✅ No trust lines, offers, escrows, checks, etc.
Destination funded ✅ Must already exist and meet base reserve
XRP balance ≥ owner reserve + fee ✅ Enough to burn reserve + tx fee
📝 Important Notes:
  • Requires the AccountDelete amendment (also known as fixRemoveAccountObjects or similar) to be enabled on the network.
  • The transaction **burns** at least the account's owner reserve (~0.2 XRP per object + base) as part of the fee — this XRP is permanently destroyed, not sent to destination.
  • All remaining XRP (after burning fee/reserve) is sent to the Destination address — no partial amounts; it's all or nothing.
  • Before deletion succeeds, **all owned ledger objects** must be removed (trust lines, offers, payment channels, escrows, checks, signer lists, DID, credentials, MPT issuances/holds, etc.).
  • The destination must be a funded account (already activated with base reserve) — many exchanges do **not** accept XRP from AccountDelete transactions.
  • Once successful, the account address is **permanently removed** from the ledger — it can never be reused or recovered.

⚠️ Permanent & Irreversible Action

Account deletion is final — the account and all history tied to it are gone forever. Use extreme caution.

  • There is **no undo** — once the transaction is included in a validated ledger, the account ceases to exist and cannot be recreated at the same address.
  • If the destination is incorrect or unfunded/incompatible (e.g., exchange deposit address), your remaining XRP will be **permanently lost**.
  • Many centralized services (exchanges, wallets) **do not support** receiving XRP from AccountDelete — confirm with the recipient first.
  • Deleting an account with valuable objects (trust lines holding IOUs, escrows, etc.) is impossible until those are cleared — attempting without cleanup will fail (tec codes like tecOWNERS).
  • Burned XRP (fee + reserves) is **permanently removed from circulation** — this contributes to XRP deflation but means you lose that amount forever.
  • Always test the full cleanup + deletion sequence on devnet or Testnet first — mainnet mistakes are irreversible and costly.
