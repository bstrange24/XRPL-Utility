Important Notes

  • All generation and derivation is client-side only — secrets never leave your browser.
  • ed25519: Faster signing, seeds start with sEd.
  • secp256k1: Bitcoin-style, seeds start with s.
  • Mnemonics and Secret Numbers are always secp256k1 to match Xumm/Xaman and common standards.
  • Family seeds support both algorithms — always specify the correct one to avoid address mismatches.
  • Generated wallets include the classic r... address and secret seed — back them up securely offline immediately.
  • Removing a wallet from this app only clears local storage — it does not affect the on-ledger account.

Critical Security & Irreversibility Warnings

Your seed, mnemonic, or secret numbers grant full control of the account. Exposure = permanent loss of funds.

  • Never share your seed, mnemonic, or secret numbers with anyone.
  • Lost secrets = permanent loss of access. No one (including Ripple or this app) can recover them.
  • Algorithm mismatch produces a different address — always double-check the address before funding.
  • Do not generate or derive wallets on shared, public, or potentially compromised devices.
  • Mnemonics and Secret Numbers cannot be used with ed25519 — they are fixed to secp256k1.
  • Test on Testnet/Devnet first when possible. There is no undo.
