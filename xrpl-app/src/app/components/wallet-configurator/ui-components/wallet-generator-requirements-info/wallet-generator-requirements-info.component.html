Generate a fresh XRPL wallet or derive an existing one from a family seed, mnemonic phrase, or secret numbers. Choose between ed25519 (faster, modern default in many libs) or secp256k1 (Bitcoin-compatible, default in rippled & Xaman/Xumm). All operations happen client-side — never share your seed, mnemonic, or numbers.

🆕 Generate New Wallet Random Seed
Algorithm ✅ Required (ed25519 or secp256k1)
Output ➕ Address + Seed (sEd... for ed25519, s... for secp256k1)
🔑 Derive Existing Wallet From Secret Input
Family Seed ➕ s... or sEd... format
Mnemonic (24 words) ➕ BIP-39 style (always secp256k1)
Secret Numbers (8×6 digits) ➕ Always secp256k1 (Xumm-compatible)
Algorithm override ❌ Limited (mnemonic/secret nums fixed to secp256k1)

📝 Important Notes

  • All derivation and generation is done client-side — your secrets never leave your device or get sent to any server.
  • ed25519: Faster signing, shorter keys (prefixed 0xED), default in many JS libs like xrpl.js — seed starts with sEd.
  • secp256k1: Bitcoin-style, default in rippled wallet_propose and Xaman/Xumm — seed starts with s (non-ed).
  • Mnemonics (24 words) and Secret Numbers (8 groups of 6 digits) always derive secp256k1 keys to match popular wallet standards (e.g., Xumm).
  • Family seeds can use either algorithm — specify explicitly to avoid address mismatches across tools.
  • Generated/derived wallets show classic r... address + (for new) the secret seed — back it up securely offline (paper, metal, etc.).
  • Removing custom wallets only clears local dropdown entries — it does not affect on-ledger accounts or funds.

⚠️ Critical Security & Irreversibility Warnings

Your seed, mnemonic, or secret numbers grant full control over the account — exposure = permanent fund loss.

  • Never share your seed/mnemonic/secret numbers — scammers impersonate support to steal them.
  • Once generated or derived, the wallet is live on the XRPL if funded — lost secrets mean permanent loss of access (no recovery by anyone, including Ripple or this app).
  • Algorithm mismatch (e.g., deriving secp256k1 seed as ed25519) produces a different address — always verify the address matches your expected one before funding or using.
  • Do not generate wallets on untrusted/compromised devices — malware can steal secrets during generation/derivation.
  • Secret Numbers and Mnemonics are fixed to secp256k1 — you cannot force ed25519 on them.
  • Test derivation on devnet/Testnet first if possible; back up secrets immediately and securely — there is no undo for lost keys.
