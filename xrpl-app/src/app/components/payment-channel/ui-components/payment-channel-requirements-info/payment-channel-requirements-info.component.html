Payment Channels

Payment Channels enable fast, low-cost, off-ledger XRP micropayments with final settlement on the XRPL ledger.

Key Operations

🔓 Create Channel
Destination Required
Amount (XRP drops) Required
Settle Delay (seconds) Required
Public Key Required
➕ Fund / Claim / Close
Channel ID Required
Amount Required (for fund/claim)

📝 Important Notes

  • Payment Channels support XRP only.
  • Claims are cumulative — you must specify the total amount claimed so far.
  • The sender signs claims off-ledger; the destination submits them on-ledger.
  • SettleDelay defines how long the sender must wait before closing.
  • Each open channel consumes owner reserve (~0.2 XRP) until closed.
⚠️ Risks & Considerations
  • Funds in a channel are locked until claimed or the channel is closed.
  • Always verify signatures and Channel IDs before claiming.
  • Test thoroughly on Devnet/Testnet first.
