📝 Important Notes:
  • Payment Channels support XRP only.
  • Claims are cumulative — each claim must specify the total amount authorized so far.
  • The sender signs off-ledger claims; the destination submits them on-ledger.
  • SettleDelay defines how long the sender must wait after requesting closure.
  • If CancelAfter is set, the channel can be closed after that time regardless of SettleDelay.
  • Each Payment Channel increases the sender’s owner reserve while open.
