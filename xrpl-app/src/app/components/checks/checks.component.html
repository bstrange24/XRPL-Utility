Check Requirements

XRPL Checks allow a sender to authorize a future payment that the recipient must actively
claim.

📝 Create Check
Destination ✅ Required
SendMax ✅ Required
Expiration ➕ Optional
💰 Cash Check
CheckID ✅ Required
Amount OR DeliverMin ✅ One Required
❌ Cancel Check
CheckID ✅ Required
📝 Important Notes:
  • A Check does not move funds until it is cashed.
  • Funds remain in the sender’s account until
    the Check is cashed.
  • The sender must have sufficient balance
    at the time of cashing.
  • If Expiration is set, the Check cannot
    be cashed after that time.
  • Each Check object increases the sender’s
    owner reserve until it is cashed or canceled.
