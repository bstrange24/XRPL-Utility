XRPL Tickets

Tickets reserve sequence numbers in advance, allowing transactions to be sent out-of-order or in parallel without depending on the account's current Sequence.

How Tickets Work

🎟️ Ticket Creation
TicketCount Required (1-250)
Max per account 250 Tickets
🎟️ Using a Ticket
Sequence Set to 0
TicketSequence Required

📝 Important Notes

  • TicketCount is required in a TicketCreate transaction (1 to 250).
  • Each unused Ticket counts toward your account’s owner reserve (typically 0.2 XRP per Ticket).
  • Tickets allow out-of-order execution — you can use any available TicketSequence in any order.
  • Maximum 250 Tickets per account at any time.
⚠️ Reserve Cost & Cleanup
  • Each Ticket increases your owner reserve — cost adds up quickly.
  • Once used, the Ticket is removed and the reserved XRP is freed.
  • Unused Tickets remain forever unless your account Sequence surpasses them.
  • Plan your TicketCount carefully to avoid locking unnecessary XRP.
